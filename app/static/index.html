How Fieldlist works

Fieldlist runs a local headless browser session directly on your machine. It browses Google Maps just like a human operator—no expensive Places APIs or subscription credits required.

    Specify target trade & city

    Include state or postal code for precise boundary resolution.

    Isolate lead opportunities & export

    Filter businesses with zero linked websites, view full profile information, or export everything to clean CSV format.
